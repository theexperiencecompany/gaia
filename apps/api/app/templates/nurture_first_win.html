Quick tip for your first day. Don't treat GAIA like a chatbot you ask questions to. Give it an actual job and let it do the work.

Here are a few things people try first. Feel free to steal one, word for word:

“Go through the emails I haven't read this week and tell me which ones actually need a reply.”

“Find me 45 free minutes tomorrow and block it for deep work.”

“Research the best options for something you're deciding on right now and give me a quick comparison.”

It takes about five minutes, and you'll get one real thing off your plate. That's the whole point of GAIA.
