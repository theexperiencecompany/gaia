One thing a lot of people don't realize: GAIA lives wherever you already are.

Link WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack or Discord and you can just text GAIA like you'd text a friend. Ask it to move a meeting while you're in line for coffee. Fire off a “remind me about this tonight” from the couch.

It works the other way too. When GAIA finishes something or needs a quick approval, it messages you there instead of waiting for you to open the app.

Takes about a minute to link, and it's honestly how most of us on the team use GAIA day to day.
