Pick where GAIA reaches you when something needs your attention.

GAIA notifies you about AI-created drafts, events, and tasks, reminders, workflow results, and system updates. Many notifications have action buttons.

## Choose your channels

<Steps>
  <Step title="Link your accounts">
    To get Telegram or Discord notifications, link them first in **Settings > Linked Accounts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Notifications">
    Go to **Settings > Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle channels">
    Flip the switch next to each channel. Changes save automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Channel           | Where it appears                          | Notes                                       |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **In-app**        | Notification panel in the web and mobile apps | Always on. Can't be turned off.         |
| **Telegram**      | Direct message from the GAIA bot          | Needs a linked account.                     |
| **Discord**       | Direct message from the GAIA bot          | Needs a linked account.                     |
| **Push (mobile)** | Native phone notification                 | Needs permission on your device.            |

<Tip>
  Keep one external channel on so you hear from GAIA outside the app.
</Tip>

## Act on notifications

| Source             | Buttons                                           |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Email draft**    | Preview & Send                                    |
| **Calendar event** | Confirm Event                                     |
| **New task**       | View Task, Mark Complete                          |
| **Workflow**       | More Details                                      |

Buttons that write data run once, then disable. If one fails, retry from the relevant page.

## Reduce noise

Select several notifications in the panel to mark them read or archive them. Archived ones leave the default view but stay accessible.

## Mobile push

The GAIA mobile app is in early access. [Join the waitlist](https://heygaia.notion.site/307023640e7b802ca404dee12cb04e89?pvs=105).

On first open, the app asks for notification permission. Accept, and your phone registers automatically. Tapping a push opens what triggered it.

<Warning>
  If you denied permission, turn notifications on in your device's system settings for GAIA.
</Warning>

Each account can register up to 10 devices. Signing out of the app on a device unregisters it.
