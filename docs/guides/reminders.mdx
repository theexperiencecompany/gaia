Tell GAIA what to remember and when, and it notifies you at the right time.

/>

Reminders work on web, mobile, desktop, and your connected bots (Discord, Slack, Telegram).

## One-time reminders

| What you say                                     | When it fires                             |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| "Remind me to call the dentist in 30 minutes"    | 30 minutes from now                       |
| "Remind me to buy groceries at 5 PM today"       | Today at 5:00 PM                          |
| "Remind me to renew my subscription on March 20" | March 20 at the time you send the message |

## Recurring reminders

| What you say                                                | Schedule                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| "Remind me to stand up and stretch every 2 hours"           | Every 2 hours                    |
| "Every Monday at 9 AM, remind me to review my weekly goals" | Weekly on Mondays at 9:00 AM     |
| "On the first of every month, remind me to pay rent"        | Monthly on the 1st               |
| "Every weekday at 6 PM, remind me to wrap up for the day"   | Monday through Friday at 6:00 PM |

To set a limit, say when to stop: "every 3 days, 10 times total" or "every Tuesday at noon until June 30".

<Note>
  Recurring reminders end 180 days after you create them unless you set an end date.
</Note>

## Manage reminders

Ask in chat to list, update, pause, resume, or cancel.

<Chat
  messages={[
    { from: "user", text: "pause my weekday wrap-up reminder" },
    { from: "gaia", text: "Paused, it won't fire until you resume it." },
    { from: "user", text: "resume it" },
    { from: "gaia", text: "Resumed, the next one fires tomorrow." },
  ]}
/>

Also try "show me my upcoming reminders" or "cancel my Monday goals reminder".

<Warning>
  Cancelled reminders cannot be restored. Pause instead if you might want it back.
</Warning>

## Timezones

GAIA uses your local timezone. To use another, say so: "remind me to join the call at 2 PM EST tomorrow".

## Reminders vs. workflows

Use a reminder for a nudge. Use a [workflow](/guides/building-workflows) when you want GAIA to act for you, like sending a summary email.
