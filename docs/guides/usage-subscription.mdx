See how much of your plan you've used, and upgrade or manage billing.

GAIA has two plans. **Free** includes every feature with usage limits. **Pro** raises those limits.

## Check your usage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Usage">
    Click your avatar in the bottom-left corner, then **Settings > Usage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a period">
    Toggle **Daily** or **Monthly**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the bars">
    Each feature shows usage against its limit. Green means plenty left, yellow means close, red means near or at the cap.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img className="rounded-lg" src="/images/guides/usage/usage-page.png" alt="Usage page showing daily limits and progress bars for each feature" />
</Frame>

Daily limits reset at midnight UTC. Monthly limits reset on the first of the month.

When you hit a limit, GAIA says which one and when it resets. Wait for the reset or upgrade to Pro.

## Upgrade to Pro

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open plans">
    Go to **Settings > Subscription** and click **View plans**, or click **Upgrade Now** on the Usage page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a cycle">
    Pick **Monthly** or **Yearly**. The modal shows the yearly savings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Click **Get Started** on the Pro card and complete checkout. Discount codes apply there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your plan updates right away. **Settings > Subscription** should show **Active**.

Pro also includes priority support and access to private Discord channels.

## Manage your subscription

**Settings > Subscription** shows your plan, status, price, next billing date, and a **Subscription ID** for support. Click **View plans** to switch between monthly and yearly.

Payments go through Dodo Payments. GAIA never stores your card details.

## Cancel

Contact GAIA support to cancel.

<Warning>
  You keep Pro until the end of your current billing period.
</Warning>
