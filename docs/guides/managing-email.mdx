Read, search, reply to, and organize Gmail from chat.

## Connect Gmail

You only do this once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Go to **Settings > Integrations** and find **Gmail**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and authorize">
    Click **Connect**, sign in to Google, and grant access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

More detail: [Connecting Integrations](/guides/connecting-integrations).

More to try:

- "show me my latest 5 emails"
- "do I have any unread emails from this week?"
- "show me the full thread with David about the partnership deal"

## Draft and send

GAIA saves a Gmail draft for you to review. It sends only when you say so.

<Chat
  messages={[
    { from: "user", text: "reply to Sarah's email saying I'll have the report by Friday" },
    { from: "gaia", text: "I've saved a draft in Gmail. Want me to send it?" },
    { from: "user", text: "make it shorter and more casual" },
    { from: "gaia", text: "Done, the draft is updated." },
    { from: "user", text: "ok, send it" },
    { from: "gaia", text: "Sent." },
  ]}
/>

You can also say "show me my drafts", "update my draft to Sarah", or "delete the draft I just created".

## Organize

- "trash the marketing email from this morning"
- "restore the email I just trashed"
- "create a label called 'Client Projects'"
- "add the 'Urgent' label to the last 3 emails from my manager"
- "find the email address for Jessica Chen" (searches your Google contacts)

## Automate your inbox

Use a workflow with the Gmail **Poll Inbox** trigger to triage new mail on a schedule. It checks every 15 minutes by default. See [Building Workflows](/guides/building-workflows).

## Get better results

- Name people and topics: "the thread with Alex about the API migration".
- Chain tools: "Check my calendar for tomorrow, then email the attendees the agenda".
