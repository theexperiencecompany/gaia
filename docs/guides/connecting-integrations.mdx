Connect an app once, and GAIA can read from it and act in it from chat.

## Connect an app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Go to **Settings > Integrations**. You can also expand **Integrations** in the chat sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Find the app and click **Connect**. Most apps open their authorization page in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    Review the permissions and click **Allow**. You land back in GAIA.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A green dot on the card means it's connected.

## Use it

No extra setup. Just ask.

<Chat
  messages={[
    { from: "user", text: "Find all unread emails from this week about the Q3 report" },
    { from: "gaia", text: "Found 4 unread emails about the Q3 report from this week." },
  ]}
/>

See the [Integrations overview](/integrations) for what's available.

## Disconnect or reconnect

Open the integration and click **Disconnect**. GAIA loses access immediately.

If a connection errors, disconnect and connect again. This fixes most expired-token problems.

## Need a tool that isn't listed?

Browse the [community marketplace](https://heygaia.io/marketplace) first. To connect your own, see [Adding MCP Integrations](/guides/adding-mcp-integrations).
