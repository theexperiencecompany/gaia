GAIA remembers things about you across conversations, so you stop repeating yourself.

You never have to say "remember this". GAIA picks up what matters on its own.

<Chat
  messages={[
    { from: "user", text: "I just moved to Austin" },
    { from: "gaia", text: "Got it, I've updated your location. Is there anything you need help with in Austin?" },
  ]}
/>

When something changes, just say so. GAIA replaces the old fact.

## See what GAIA knows

Open **Settings > Memories**. It has four tabs.

| Tab           | What it shows                                                                                       |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Facts**     | Single facts, grouped into topic folders. Example: "Your birthday is March 14th".                   |
| **Journal**   | A short summary of each day you talked to GAIA.                                                     |
| **Documents** | Living notes GAIA reads at the start of every conversation: `user.md`, `memory.md`, `people.md`, `agenda.md`. |
| **Graph**     | A map of the people, places, and projects in your life and how they connect.                        |

## Fix or remove memories

- **Edit** a fact or document that is wrong.
- **Delete** any single memory.
- **Clear all** to start fresh.

<Warning>
  Clearing all memories cannot be undone.
</Warning>

Your memories are private to your account.

<Accordion title="More on each tab">
  Documents are auto-written, and you can edit them with **Edit**.

  - **user.md**: who you are, where you work and live, how you like to communicate
  - **memory.md**: a summary of what GAIA has learned
  - **people.md**: the people in your life and how they connect
  - **agenda.md**: your current priorities

  The graph supports zoom, pan, fit to screen, and export as PNG or SVG.
