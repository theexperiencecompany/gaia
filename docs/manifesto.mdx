## Every Human Deserves a Jarvis

Imagine an assistant who knows your work, your patterns, and your preferences. It reads your email first and surfaces what matters. It schedules your day, drafts your replies, and researches before you ask.

It doesn't wait for commands. It anticipates. It gets smarter the longer it works with you.

That is GAIA. Every person should have an assistant like Jarvis from Iron Man. Not in fifty years, and not for a privileged few. Now, for everyone.

## The Problem

We all drown in tools: Gmail, Calendar, Todos, Docs, Slack, Linear, WhatsApp. Our days fill with small, repetitive actions that feel necessary but aren't real work.

Each one is small. Together they are crushing. Inboxes clutter, todo lists rot, and important things slip through.

That's how this started for us. We missed a time-sensitive email because everything looked equally urgent. One miss cascaded into missed opportunities and frustrated partners. We had become servants to our tools.

Existing tools don't fix this. Siri sets timers. Alexa plays music. ChatGPT answers questions but can't act for you. Zapier is powerful but rigid, and it makes you think like a programmer.

A real assistant has agency, memory, and initiative. That's what we're building.

## Why Now

Language models can now understand context, reason through problems, and write like people. The tools to integrate with every digital service exist. Knowledge graphs can connect and store what you know.

The technology is ready. What was missing was the vision to combine it, and the commitment to do it right, with privacy, transparency, and you in control.

## How We Build It

GAIA is a General-purpose AI Assistant. It connects to your digital life, learns how you work, and handles the repetitive parts.

- **Deep integration.** Email, calendar, todos, chat tools, and knowledge bases, seen the way a human assistant would see them.
- **Persistent memory.** A graph that connects tasks to projects, meetings to documents, and people to topics.
- **Proactive intelligence.** GAIA watches deadlines and important email, and acts before you prompt it.
- **Intelligent action.** It sends emails, schedules meetings, triages your inbox, and runs multi-step workflows.
- **Privacy by architecture.** An assistant that knows everything about you needs absolute trust.

That is why GAIA is open source, so every line is auditable. It is why GAIA is self-hostable, so your data lives where you want it. And it is why we will never sell your information or train models on it.

## The Journey Ahead

We start with knowledge workers, who value their time most. We solve the hardest problems first, then expand.

The end goal is GAIA on every device for every person: mobile, desktop, and web.

If you believe everyone deserves a Jarvis, join us. Use GAIA. Contribute code. Share feedback. Tell others.

The future doesn't wait. Let's build it together.
